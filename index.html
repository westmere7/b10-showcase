Getting around

Look around

  • Rotate: drag with the left mouse button, or one finger
  • Drag the view: middle or right mouse button (or ⌘/Ctrl+drag), or two fingers
  • Move forward / back: scroll wheel, pinch, or + / −
  • Go to a school: click the open floor of its coloured zone
  • Focus one stand: click it (hovering outlines it)
  • Or use the buttons at the bottom right

Top view

  • Looks straight down: drag to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a stand or zone to frame it
  • Isometric (flat, to scale) or Perspective: buttons at the bottom right, or Space

Mac trackpad

  • Rotate: click and drag (or twist two fingers in Safari)
  • Drag the view: swipe with two fingers
  • Move forward / back: pinch

Walk mode (1.7 m eye level)

  • Look: click the view once, then just move the mouse (touch: drag)
  • Walk to a spot: click to walk to the crosshair (touch: tap the floor)
  • Move: WASD or the arrow keys; hold Shift to go faster. Trackpad: two-finger swipe up/down to walk
  • Zoom: mouse wheel or + / − widens or narrows the view
  • Fly: E up / Q down, from eye level to 12 m
  • Exit: Esc frees the mouse; press Esc again (or choose any view) to leave

Shortcuts

  • 1 SSET · 2 SCD · 3 TBS
  • Esc back to Overview · Space top view (again: isometric ⇄ perspective)
  • F walk mode · T hall tour · L shadows on/off
  • C save a photo · V record a video path (K keyframe, Q/E fly down/up)
  • Any click or key stops a tour
